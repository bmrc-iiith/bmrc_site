Biomedical Research Center (BMRC)

A multidisciplinary initiative at the intersection of biology, engineering, and technology, addressing pressing medical and healthcare research questions.

Explore Research Areas Meet the Faculty

About BMRC

The BMRC fosters collaborative research across departments to develop technologies and scientific insights for prevention, diagnosis, intervention, and rehabilitation. The group's expertise spans sensor development, wearables, brain imaging, computational biology, assistive systems (hardware/software) and bio-materials. We collaborate with clinicians and labs.
